## 📌 Project Overview

Financial dashboard built in **Power BI Desktop** to analyze sales performance by product, country, and market segment. The report is split into two main pages:

### Page 1 – Sales Report by Product and Segment
Sales analysis segmented by product and distribution channel.

### Page 2 – Sales Report by Country and Profit
Profit and revenue analysis by country with temporal trend tracking.
